A UX review for TemplatesOn

I recorded myself using TemplatesOn. Here's what I saw.

3 things working, 3 I'd tighten.

The good

What's working

Clean first impression. The hero says what it is in one line, no jargon.

The template previews are the star. Showing real output up front builds trust fast.

Fast to try. I could start browsing without hitting a signup wall.

The gaps

3 things I'd tighten

01

Your pricing hides the paid tiers.

The page shows a free tier but the paid plans sit behind a click. Someone deciding if this fits their budget has to go hunting. Put the plans on the page, side by side.

02

The top nav is carrying 20+ items.

The menu tries to expose everything at once, so nothing stands out. Group it down to the 4-5 things a first-time visitor actually needs.

03

A feature block repeats in the carousel.

The same card shows up twice as you scroll, which makes the page feel longer than it is and dilutes the strong stuff. Cut the duplicate.
