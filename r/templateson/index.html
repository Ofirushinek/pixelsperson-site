3 things I'd tighten

01

The hero subhead does good work, but it stacks four tools in a row and my brain glazes a bit - try leading with the pain-point use case first, then drop the integrations as proof: 'Auto-generate invoices and social images from Zapier, n8n…'

02

That cyan accent pill on every section heading ('BULK RENDERING', 'QR, BARCODES…') - it's working overtime and starting to feel like wallpaper. Pick one or two hero moments to badge, let the rest breathe without the highlighter.

03

'Built for automated output' as a heading is technically true but it's also… forgettable? You've got rocket fuel in 'Wire up your data once and let finished files generate themselves' - promote that thought up, make it the headline, and watch it land harder.
