3 things I'd tighten

01

Your pricing hides the paid tiers.

The page shows a free tier but the paid plans sit behind a click. Someone deciding if this fits their budget has to go hunting. Put the plans on the page, side by side.

Why it mattersSomeone checking if this fits their budget has to go hunting for the price, and a lot of them just leave instead.

Suggested fixPut the 2-3 plans on the page as cards, free tier first, with prices visible before any click.

02

The top nav is carrying 20+ items.

The menu tries to expose everything at once, so nothing stands out. Group it down to the 4-5 things a first-time visitor actually needs.

Why it mattersA packed menu makes a first-time visitor work to find the one thing they actually came for.

Suggested fixGroup the nav down to the 4-5 items that matter most and tuck the rest under a single "More" menu.

03

A feature block repeats in the carousel.

The same card shows up twice as you scroll, which makes the page feel longer than it is and dilutes the strong stuff. Cut the duplicate.

Why it mattersThe repeat makes the page feel longer and padded, which quietly lowers trust in the strong parts.

Suggested fixRemove the duplicate card so each feature in the carousel appears exactly once.
