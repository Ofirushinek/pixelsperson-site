I recorded myself using Coast.
Here's what I saw.

Coast turns your iPhone into a custom control surface for your Mac - buttons, sliders, macros, all built on your phone's screen. This is Remote Desktop's cooler younger sibling, and the product hook is genuinely clever.

The good

Three things I loved

The gaps

3 things I'd tighten

01

That opening hero section is gorgeous, but it fills the whole screen and all I see is a big logo and the tagline. I'd surface one concrete example of what this does - a single sentence like 'Control Photoshop, trigger shortcuts, scrub timelines' - before I scroll. Clarity first.

02

The 'anything.' fragment at the top of the second section leans too hard on mystery. I scrolled past it twice before I understood what 'anything' referred to. Something like 'Build layouts for any workflow' says the same thing with way less guesswork.

03

The 'Every control clicks back. You feel the Mac respond' line is selling tactile feedback, but it reads vague - I can't tell if it's literal haptics or just snappy UI. I'd be explicit: 'Haptic feedback on every tap', or whatever the real feature is.

This is a sharp product page for a product that deserves one. Tighten the clarity up top and you've got something that converts.
