The sprint

Assess, Shape, Build

One sequence, about six weeks end to end. Stages can also be taken on their own.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Product Gap Review · ~1 week

    An audit against your business goals: user flows, onboarding, information architecture, launch readiness. You get a prioritized gap map with annotated screenshots, severity and impact per issue, and clear next steps. Not a deck. A working document you can act on the same day.

  2. 02

    Shape

    Product Definition · ~2 weeks

    Findings become decisions. Flows, spec, requirements, scope, design-system fit. A Product Blueprint ready to hand to whoever builds it: your team, a freelancer, or me.

  3. 03

    Build

    AI-Assisted Prototype · ~3 weeks

    A working, testable, demoable prototype built with AI tools and your existing components. Production-minded prototyping: fast, but built to be believed, not a mockup.
